Calculation Methodology & Emission Factors

Peer-reviewed sources, official Indian baselines, and transparent modeling rules

1. Baseline Emission Factors (kg CO2e / km)

  • Walking / Cycling: 0.000 kg CO2e/km (Zero direct tailpipe emissions).
  • Namma Metro (BMRCL): 0.018 kg CO2e/pkm (Electric rail on Karnataka grid, regenerative braking, high passenger capacity).
  • BMTC Bus: 0.028 kg CO2e/pkm (Fleet average of BMTC non-AC, AC Vajra, and EV buses divided across average 40 passengers).
  • Auto Rickshaw: 0.065 kg CO2e/pkm (4-stroke LPG/CNG 3-wheeler in Bengaluru city traffic, avg 1.3 occupants).
  • Two-Wheeler: 0.048 kg CO2e/pkm (100-150cc petrol commuter motorcycle/scooter, solo rider).
  • Personal Car: 0.155 kg CO2e/pkm (Urban ICE petrol/diesel car with 1.2 average occupancy).
  • Cab (Uber/Ola): 0.185 kg CO2e/pkm (Includes 20% deadheading mileage cruising without passenger).

2. Household Electricity (BESCOM Grid)

Grid emission factor: 0.716 kg CO2e / kWh.
Derived from the Central Electricity Authority (CEA) CO2 Baseline Database for the Indian Power Sector (User Guide v19.0) for Southern Regional Grid, factoring Karnataka’s mix of Pavagada solar, wind, Sharavathi hydro, and Raichur/Kudgi thermal power.

Household consumption is divided per-capita: Personal kWh = Total Household kWh / Household Members.

3. Bengaluru Rush-Hour Congestion Penalty

Bengaluru traffic (Silk Board, Outer Ring Road, Tin Factory) frequently slows average speeds to 10-14 km/h. Idle engine running and repeated acceleration increases fuel consumption:

  • Car & Cab: Up to +35% penalty at 100% peak congestion.
  • Auto Rickshaw: Up to +25% penalty.
  • Two-Wheeler: Up to +20% penalty.
  • BMTC Bus: Up to +15% penalty.
  • Namma Metro & Active mobility: 0% penalty (unaffected by road traffic).

4. Vehicle Ageing & Model Fallbacks

Older vehicles experience catalytic converter efficiency loss, BS-norm differences (BS3/BS4 vs BS6), and mechanical wear. Modeled at +2.0% additional emission per year (capped at +30%).

Transparent ML Fallback: If ML artifacts are uninitialized or missing, the system automatically falls back to this deterministic Tier-1 calculator with zero user interruption.
